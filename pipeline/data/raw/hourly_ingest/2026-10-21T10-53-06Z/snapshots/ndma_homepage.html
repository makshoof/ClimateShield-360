About NDMA

To build a resilient Pakistan through proactive disaster risk reduction, effective preparedness, and coordinated response — protecting lives, livelihoods, and infrastructure from natural and man-made disasters.

View
NEOC Wing

Technical Early Warning

Tracking global oceanic and atmospheric oscillations to assess local hazards. Our team operates 24/7 to ensure timely warnings and proactive disaster risk management across Pakistan.

View
NIDM Wing

NIDM National Think Tank

The National Institute of Disaster Management serves as Pakistan's premier knowledge hub for disaster risk reduction, training professionals and developing policy frameworks.

View
DRR Wing

Disasters Risk Reduction

Implementing Pakistan's commitment to the Sendai Framework through evidence-based policies and community-level disaster preparedness programmes across all provinces.

View
Media Wing

Media Group National & Global

Coordinating national and international media engagement to disseminate critical disaster alerts, public service messages, and awareness campaigns across all platforms.

View
IA Wing

Infrastructure Advisory and Project Development

Providing technical guidance on disaster-resilient construction, infrastructure protection, and post-disaster damage assessment to government bodies and private entities.

View
Operations Wing

Operations & Logistics

Coordinating rapid-response operations, relief distribution, and logistical support during active disaster events across Pakistan's provinces and territories.

View
Plans Wing

Plans Coordination

Plans Wing develops the National Disaster Management Plan (NDMP), integrating anticipatory action components, seasonal preparedness and national DRR priorities. It coordinates with NGOs, INGOs, UN agencies, and development partners for proactive disaster management, preparedness and response planning.

View
NR Wing

National Resource Coordination

Mobilising national resources, public-private partnerships, and diaspora networks to build and sustain financial resilience for disaster risk management across Pakistan.

View
IC Wing

International Diplomatic Coordination

Building strategic partnerships with global organisations and the European Union to enhance Pakistan's disaster resilience.

View
RMC Wing

Regional & Military Coordination

Fostering regional and military collaboration in disaster management and risk reduction, acting as a conduit between NDMA and its counterparts in friendly countries.

View
CoE / NIDM

Centre of Excellence C2DM

The CoE conducts the flagship Climate Change & Disaster Management Programme, building institutional capacity through specialised courses for civil and military officers.

View
Risk Finance

Risk Finance Wing

Pakistan's national platform for climate and disaster risk financing, supporting sovereign risk transfer mechanisms, fiscal risk analytics, and resilience investment pathways.
