What the System Does NOT Claim (Explicit Safety Boundaries)

Mandatory Non-Claims Disclosure
gavel No Legal or Evidentiary Certification

Modelled flood inundation footprints, rainfall millimeters, and thermal stress indices generated by this platform cannot be submitted as legally binding certifications for property flood damage compensation, insurance payout litigation, or civil contractor indemnity claims without independent physical post-event field audit conducted by an authorized Sindh Revenue or KMC engineering officer.

block Non-admissible in tribunal absent certified field ledger
roofing No Micro-Level Indoor Thermal Guarantee

Temperature projections represent outdoor ambient Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) calculated in free-flowing shade. High-density residential settlements with uninsulated corrugated iron (tin) roofing, lack of cross-ventilation, and localized masonry thermal retention (e.g. densely packed quarters in Machar Colony, Lyari, or Korangi) will routinely exceed modeled outdoor values by +4°C to +8°C.

thermostat Indoor microclimates require direct manual spot measurement
power_off No Automated Infrastructure Shutdowns

ClimateShield 360 operates strictly as an advisory decision-support layer. All physical mitigation actions require explicit human operator intervention following formal command protocols.

Scientific Methodology & Mathematical Architecture

Core Hydrometeorological Engines
MODEL ENG-01 Resolution: 1.2km

Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP)

Downscaled Weather Research and Forecasting (WRF-ARW v4.2) modeling framework initialized quad-daily (00, 06, 12, 18 UTC). Boundary conditions are derived from ECMWF IFS (0.1°) paired with NOAA GFS (0.25°) ensemble inputs.

Governing Schemes
  • Microphysics: Morrison 2-Moment Scheme
  • Surface Layer: Monin-Obukhov Similarity (Janjic)
  • Urban Canopy: Single-Layer UCM (Noah-LSM)
MODEL ENG-02 Cadence: 10 min

Wet-Bulb Globe Temp (WBGT) Engine

Empirical formulation combining ambient 2m dry-bulb temperature (Ta), relative humidity (RH), direct solar radiation flux (S), and 10m wind velocity vector (v) calibrated using Liljegren’s validated atmospheric heat exchange equations.

Mathematical Approximation:
WBGT = 0.7·Tw + 0.2·Tg + 0.1·Td
Tw: Natural Wet-Bulb | Tg: Globe Temp | Td: Dry-Bulb
MODEL ENG-03 Calibrated: 2020 & 2022

Karachi Urban Runoff Model (KURM)

Two-dimensional shallow water equation (SWE) hydraulic flood-routing across 18 municipal zones. Accounts for surface imperviousness fractions, digital terrain models (SRTM + Drone LiDAR samples), and tidal backwater at KPT/Manora harbor outfalls.

Empirical Ground-Truthing Benchmarked against high-water marks from the August 27, 2020 catastrophic monsoon downpour (230mm/12h at PAF Faisal) and July 2022 Malir River overflow benchmarks.

Known Computational Limitations & Uncertainty Propagation

Hydrometeorological forecasting in Karachi is bounded by specific micro-geographic anomalies. Municipal teams must factor these documented limitations into operational deployment planning:

air Coastal Breeze Microfront Inversions

Rapid onshore wind shifts from the Arabian Sea create localized temperature drops of up to 3.2°C within 15 minutes in Clifton, DHA, and Kemari. Global numerical boundary sets (GFS) fail to resolve the exact timing of this sea-breeze penetration front, leading to temporary over-estimation of heat stress near coastal sectors between 13:00 and 15:30 PKT.

waves Dynamic Nullah Sedimentation & Solid Waste Drift

The carrying capacities of Gujjar Nullah, Orangi Nullah, and Nehr-e-Khayyam are not static hydraulic constants. Illegal municipal solid waste accumulation, temporary construction scaffolding, and localized sediment silting can reduce cross-sectional discharge rates by 25% to 55% without sensor detection until physical backwater occurs.

water_lux High Spring Tide Backwater Stagnation

During lunar spring tides (>3.4m KPT datum), Karachi South's coastal gravity drains experience hydraulic lock. Rainfall cannot discharge into Chinna Creek or the Lyari delta regardless of channel clearance, causing instantaneous ponding in Kharadar, Mithadar, and II Chundrigar road sectors that hydraulic models map as free-flowing.

satellite_alt Dense Cloud Attenuation on Low-Earth Orbit Radar

Severe convective tropical storms can introduce optical and microwave backscatter degradation. Sentinel-1 SAR and PMD S-band radar reflections can suffer from beam attenuation in rainfall exceeding 65mm/hr, creating temporary radar shadow zones across northern Gadap and Malir districts.
